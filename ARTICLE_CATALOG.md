# Article Catalog — kacatempered.co.id

This catalog plans a complete Indonesian safety-glazing knowledge system for KacaTempered.co.id. It expands every parent topic into eight distinct intents while keeping the first publication wave deliberately limited.

Domain boundaries: neutral guides explain tempered and safety-glass decisions, risks, evidence, and lifecycle tasks. Existing `/produk/*` and location routes own service and quotation intent. Another owned domain may cover the same query independently; anti-cannibalization controls here apply only within KacaTempered.co.id.

Catalog statistics: 16 topics, 128 planned articles, eight articles per topic, and 12 Wave 1 assets. All thickness, load, railing, canopy, overhead glass, facade, hardware, breakage, and installation-safety articles require the specialist gates in `TOPICAL_AUTHORITY.md`.

Priority legend: `P0` is the bounded first cluster; `P1` is the next connected authority layer; `P2` expands application and lifecycle depth; `P3` is specialist or lower-demand coverage that follows evidence and capacity.
