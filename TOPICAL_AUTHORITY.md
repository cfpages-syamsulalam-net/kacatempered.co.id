# Topical Authority — kacatempered.co.id

## Role and boundary

KacaTempered.co.id is an Indonesian knowledge and commercial-support property about tempered and safety glazing for buildings, interiors, furniture, and specialist applications. Its core reader is a homeowner, building owner, buyer, architect, contractor, facility manager, or fabricator who must understand a glazing decision before requesting a quotation or accepting installed work.

The editorial boundary is safety-glass selection, specification, fabrication, installation interfaces, inspection, maintenance, and failure response. The site may independently cover subjects also discussed by other Syamsul-owned domains; cannibalization is evaluated only among pages on KacaTempered.co.id. Existing `/produk/*` and location routes own transactional service intent. New articles must remain educational or decision-supportive and link to a commercial route only when contextually useful.

Safety-critical pages are not substitutes for project engineering, licensed professional design, manufacturer instructions, site-specific risk assessment, or competent installation. No article may prescribe glass thickness, panel size, support spacing, hole position, fixing, anchor, load capacity, or fall-protection detail from a generic rule of thumb.

## Existing coverage and risks

- Sitemap volume overstates knowledge coverage: 852 of 894 URLs are location variants and must not be treated as distinct expertise.
- The same service entities recur across location, `/produk/*`, homepage, and media routes. New neutral guides need explicit boundaries so they do not compete with commercial pages.
- Eight `-2` pairs are likely duplication or legacy-canonical problems and require indexing, backlink, canonical, and content comparison before any redirect.
- Manager and WhatsApp archives include feeds and pagination even though the two primary profile paths already redirect externally through `_redirects`.
- The video sitemap multiplies nine embedded videos across 869 page entries; it needs eligibility and duplication review rather than being counted as an original-video library.
- Several legacy pages contain dated commercial language and unsupported superlatives. Technical claims, prices, and year references require refresh or removal.
- Existing content presents thicknesses as product options but does not prove that a thickness is safe for a specific panel, load, support, edge, hole, or application.
- Railing, canopy, overhead glass, facade, door, hardware, breakage, and installation advice can cause injury if generic guidance is mistaken for design approval.

## Evidence and editorial standards

### Evidence ladder

1. Applicable Indonesian law and current official BSN catalogue status.
2. Full licensed SNI or adopted ISO text, not a search snippet or reseller summary.
3. Project drawings, calculations, specifications, tests, approvals, and manufacturer instructions for the actual system.
4. Named specialist review: structural engineer, facade engineer, architect, glazing/fabrication specialist, hardware specialist, K3 professional, building-physics specialist, or procurement/legal reviewer as relevant.
5. Original measurements, photographs, mock-ups, samples, maintenance records, and documented incidents.
6. Secondary explanation only for noncritical context and only when its sources can be traced.

Official catalogue anchors confirmed during planning include [SNI 9144-2:2022](https://pesta.bsn.go.id/produk/detail/14417-sni9144-22022), [SNI 9144-1:2022](https://pesta.bsn.go.id/produk/detail/14416-sni9144-12022), [SNI 15-0131-2006](https://pesta.bsn.go.id/produk/detail/7173-sni15-0131-2006), [SNI 1727:2020](https://pesta.bsn.go.id/produk/detail/12927-sni17272020), and [SNI 1726:2019](https://pesta.bsn.go.id/produk/detail/12762-sni17262019). Catalogue status alone does not establish which edition, clause, or standard governs a particular project.

### Mandatory specialist gates

- Thickness, panel size, loads, deflection, support, hole and notch geometry: structural or facade engineer review plus current standards and manufacturer/fabricator constraints.
- Railings and fall protection: structural engineer and tested-system evidence; verify substrate, anchors, retention after breakage, and project-specific loads.
- Canopies, roofs, skylights, and overhead glass: structural/facade engineer review; address laminated retention, drainage, ponding, wind, maintenance access, and falling-fragment consequence.
- Facades and curtain walls: facade and structural engineer review; use system-specific calculations, movement design, mock-up/test evidence, weather detailing, and replacement access.
- Hardware, frames, anchors, sealants, and structural interfaces: manufacturer compatibility documents plus the responsible designer; do not infer load capacity from appearance.
- Breakage and defects: isolate the area first, preserve evidence, and obtain glazing/materials or structural review depending on consequence. Do not remotely declare a cause from a photograph alone.
- Transport, installation, and replacement: competent glazing installer and K3 plan; cutting, drilling, lifting, work at height, and structural fixing are not DIY procedures.
- Shower, door, partition, furniture, and display pages: application specialist review with user-impact, exposed-edge, hardware, cleaning, and misuse considerations.

Every technical article must show author, reviewer role, source list, review date, assumptions, limits, and a correction path. Never invent clause numbers, test results, case studies, prices, certifications, or firsthand experience.
